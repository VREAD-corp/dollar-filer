What you get

FileWhat it does
Return worksheet (.xlsx)Log each payment with the CBN rate of that day, your costs and reliefs. It totals your income, applies the six bands of the Fourth Schedule, the rent relief cap and the foreign tax credit of s.120, and gives the figures to copy into the state form.
Document checklist (PDF)Two pages of tick boxes: NIN and Tax ID, Deel or Upwork statements, domiciliary account statements, rent receipts and tenancy agreement, PFA statement. Download it free now.
State filing guide (PDF)Step by step for LIRS eTax (Lagos) and the FCT-IRS portal, how to get your 13-digit Tax ID, and the generic route for the other 35 states. Penalties in plain figures: ₦100,000 for the first month you fail to file, ₦50,000 for each month after (NTAA s.101).
Record-keeping template (.xlsx)A monthly log of every dollar, pound or euro that lands, with the evidence file name. The law asks you to keep records for six years, in English (NTAA s.31).
